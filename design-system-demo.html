Civic Tech

tags: civic-tech, governance, democracy updated: 2026-06-05

Technology built explicitly to strengthen civic participation, democratic governance, and public interest — serving communities, government, and civil society rather than extracting value or accumulating power. The 6-Pack of Care provides six design principles for building systems communities can trust.

Kami

tags: civic-ai, governance, stewardship updated: 2026-06-05

Bounded local stewards — the deployment unit for community-governed AI. Rather than one powerful system serving everyone, a Kami lets a community own and govern a scoped, auditable, sunset-ready AI steward, in keeping with the Civic.ai 6-Pack of Care.
